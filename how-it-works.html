How Unisell works

Unisell is a place for students to trade things they no longer need. Here is how each part works.

If you want to buy

  • Find it. Browse Explore, use the filters, or type a plain request such as “used calculator under 1500” in the search box.
  • Check it. Open a listing to see the condition, campus, seller and price. Save it with the heart, or add it to Compare.
  • Get it. Add it to your cart, choose free campus pickup or home delivery, and check out.

If you want to sell, rent out, exchange or donate

  • List it. Go to Sell and answer five short steps: type, details, condition and price, campus, and a final preview.
  • Manage it. Your listings appear in Explore straight away. Open Your listings to edit or unlist them at any time.

If you want to rent

  • Pick a start and end date on a rental listing. Dates that are already booked are blocked.
  • The total is the daily price times the number of days. A refundable deposit is shown on the listing.

What is a demo today

Listings from other sellers are sample data. Payments are simulated, and everything you create is saved only in your browser. Accounts, a shared database and real payments are planned for the next stage.
