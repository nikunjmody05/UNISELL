The marketplace built for campus life.

Students buy things they use for a semester, then let them sit in a cupboard. Unisell is a place to buy, sell, rent, exchange or donate those things within your university community.

You can browse 60 sample listings across six categories, list your own item in five steps, rent a calculator by the day, and check out with a simulated payment. Everything is stored in your browser for now.

Unisell is a portfolio project by Nikunj Mody, built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and instrumented with Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. Real accounts, a database, university verification and live payments are planned for the next stage.
