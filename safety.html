Trust and safety

Trading with other students works best when everyone takes a few simple precautions.

Meeting up

  • Meet on campus in a busy, well-lit place, ideally during the day.
  • Bring a friend if you can, and tell someone where you are going.
  • Check the item before you pay. Test electronics and look through books and clothes.

Payments

  • Checkout on Unisell is a simulation today, so no real money moves and you should never enter real card details.
  • Never share an OTP, PIN or card number with anyone, whatever the reason.
  • Be careful if a seller asks you to pay in advance outside the site.

Spotting a bad deal

  • Prices far below normal are a warning sign.
  • Be wary of pressure to decide quickly or to move the conversation elsewhere.
  • For rentals, agree the return date and condition before you take the item.

What is coming

Unisell does not have accounts, university verification, reviews or a way to report a listing yet. These are planned for the next stage, so please take extra care until they exist.
