Help and FAQ

Quick answers to common questions. If yours is not here, contact us.

Is Unisell a real marketplace?

It is a working front-end project. The shopping, selling, renting and checkout flows all run, but the other sellers and listings are sample data.

Are payments real?

No. Checkout is simulated and no money is taken. Please do not enter real card details.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser only. Your cart, saved items, listings and orders are not sent to a server, so clearing your browser data removes them.

How do I list an item?

Open Sell, choose a type, and follow the five steps. Your listing then appears in Explore.

How do I edit or remove my listing?

Open Sell, then Manage your listings, or go to your seller page. Each listing has Edit and Unlist.

How does renting work?

Choose a start and end date on a rental listing. Booked dates are blocked, and the total is the daily price times the days.

What happens when I request an exchange or a donated item?

You see a confirmation, but the request is not delivered yet because there are no accounts or messaging. That arrives in the next stage.

Why can I not find a listing?

Try fewer words, clear the filters, or search by category. A search needs every word to match.
