One store for the things you actually use.

Unisell started as a simple idea: shopping online should be quick, clear and honest. We bring electronics, fashion and accessories together in one place, so you don’t need five tabs to finish one purchase.

Prices are shown in rupees with no surprises at checkout. Delivery is free above ₹5,000, and anything you don’t love can go back within seven days.

Unisell is also a portfolio project by Nikunj Mody, built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and instrumented with Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to study real shopping behaviour.
